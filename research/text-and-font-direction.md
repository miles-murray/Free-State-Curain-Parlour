# Text And Font Direction

## Recommended Direction

Use a clean, quietly premium sans-serif for most headings and body text, with a refined serif used only as a light accent where it supports the established 1959 heritage.

This gives Free State Curtain Parlour the right balance:

- Established and trusted
- Elegant but not old-fashioned
- Warm and showroom-led
- Clear and readable for local customers
- Aligned with the black-and-white logo
- Distinct from the Two Furniture Reimagined reference site

## Font Pairing

### Recommended Pairing

- Headings: Avenir Next, Optima, Gill Sans, or Inter
- Body: Avenir Next, Inter, Source Sans 3, or Helvetica Neue
- Accent serif: Georgia, Cormorant Garamond, or Playfair Display, used sparingly for heritage details or small editorial moments

If using free web fonts, a strong pairing would be:

- Headings: Inter or Source Sans 3
- Body: Inter or Source Sans 3
- Accent: Cormorant Garamond

If keeping to system fonts:

- Headings: Avenir Next or Optima
- Body: Avenir Next or Helvetica Neue
- Accent: Georgia

## Colour Use With Typography

- Main text: black
- Large headings: black or charcoal
- Body copy: warm grey or black
- Section labels: charcoal, black, or aged bronze
- Buttons: black with off-white text, or soft gold with black text
- Background: off-white, not pure white
- Soft sections: linen beige or very light warm grey

## Differentiation From Two Furniture Reimagined

Two Furniture Reimagined uses a soft editorial look: large refined serif headings, off-white backgrounds, gold eyebrow labels, warm muted text, and a calm luxury layout.

Free State Curtain Parlour should not repeat that one-for-one. It should feel more like an established interiors showroom:

- More charcoal, black framing, and showroom structure
- Less cream-and-gold editorial styling
- Gold/bronze used as hardware-level detail, not a dominant accent
- Sans-serif headings for a cleaner, more grounded identity
- Serif used selectively for heritage, not as the main visual signature
- Real showroom photography, fabric, wood, curtains, rails, and decor displays should carry the warmth

## Tone Of Voice

The copy should be warm, clear, and practical. It should sound like a trusted local showroom, not a luxury hotel brand or a big online store.

Good:

> Bring your room together with curtains, blinds, furniture and decor advice from our Bloemfontein showroom.

Avoid:

> Bespoke lifestyle environments for sophisticated interiors.
