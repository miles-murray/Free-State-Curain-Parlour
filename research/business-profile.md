## How They Seem To Work

Their business appears to be showroom-led.

The likely customer journey:

1. A customer sees the shop or hears about it locally.
2. They walk into the showroom.
3. They browse furniture, curtain displays, fabrics, and decor items.
4. Staff help them choose materials, colours, textures, curtain styles, or furniture pieces.
5. For curtain/blind work, the job may move into measuring, quoting, manufacturing, delivery, and installation.
6. The final sale is likely closed through in-person advice, trust, and product inspection.

The website should therefore support the showroom, not replace it. Its job is to make people feel confident enough to visit, call, WhatsApp, or request a quote.
