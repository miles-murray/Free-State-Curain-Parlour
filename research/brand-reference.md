## Brand Implications

The card gives the business a more established and design-led feel than a simple curtain shop. "Decor" and related terms should be used as service language, not as part of the main business name unless needed in supporting copy.

The homepage should therefore emphasize:

- Established local presence since 1959
- Interior design and decor advice
- Curtains and blinds as core services
- Wallpaper as a confirmed offering
- A physical Westdene showroom
- Johan or the team as approachable points of contact
