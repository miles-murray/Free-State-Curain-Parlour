# Customer Demographic

## Primary Customer

The primary customer is a Bloemfontein or Free State homeowner who wants to improve, redecorate, furnish, or finish their home.

They are likely not searching for abstract interior design. They are usually thinking about a practical home need:

- I need new curtains.
- I need blinds for a room.
- I want to redo my lounge.
- I need a couch, cabinet, or furniture piece.
- I want my furniture and curtains to work together.
- I do not know what colour, fabric, or style to choose.
- I want to see options in person before spending money.

## Core Audience Segments

### Established Homeowners

These customers already own or live long-term in a home. They are likely refreshing bedrooms, lounges, dining rooms, or living spaces.

They care about:

- Quality
- Durability
- Helpful advice
- Seeing fabrics in person
- Finished-room inspiration
- Trust before spending on custom work

### First-Time Home Buyers

Bloemfontein has strong first-time buyer activity because the city is comparatively affordable. These customers may need curtains, couches, storage, cabinets, and basic decor after moving in.

They care about:

- Price clarity
- Practical recommendations
- Packages or room-by-room guidance
- A welcoming showroom
- Not feeling judged for having a smaller budget

### Renovation And Redecorating Customers

These customers are upgrading an existing space. They may have a clear style in mind, or they may need help matching curtains, furniture, and decor.

They care about:

- Taste
- Coordination
- Fabric and finish quality
- Before/after examples
- Advice from someone experienced

### Affluent Homeowners

These customers are more likely to want premium fabrics, custom curtains, blinds, quality furniture, and a more curated look.

They care about:

- Quality finishes
- Premium presentation
- Beautiful showroom photography
- Strong trust signals
- Personal service
- Less cluttered, more elegant website design

### Guesthouses, Rentals, Offices, And Commercial Buyers

This is a secondary audience, but worth supporting. They may need multiple curtains, blinds, furniture pieces, or soft furnishing solutions.

They care about:

- Reliability
- Bulk/project capability
- Clear contact path
- Delivery/installation
- Practical, durable products
- Professional-looking results

## Why This Is Their Market

Their shop is walk-in and showroom-driven. That means the buyer is usually local, visually motivated, and wants to physically inspect items before committing.

Curtains, blinds, and furniture are also higher-trust purchases. Customers want to know:

- Will this fit my room?
- Will the colour work?
- Will the fabric last?
- Can I trust the advice?
- Will installation be handled properly?
- Is this business established and real?

The website must reduce uncertainty before the customer visits.

## Customer Mindset

Most visitors will not arrive thinking, "I need a full interior design solution."

They will arrive thinking:

- "I need curtains."
- "I need a couch."
- "I need help choosing."
- "I am redecorating."
- "I saw the shop and want to know if it is worth visiting."

The homepage must quickly show that Free State Curtain Parlour & Decor can help them with both the product and the decision.

## Messaging Tone

The tone should be:

- Warm
- Helpful
- Local
- Clear
- Slightly premium
- Practical
- Not overly corporate
- Not too trendy or abstract

Good message:

> Bring your room together with curtains, blinds, furniture and decor advice from our Bloemfontein showroom.

Avoid:

> Bespoke lifestyle environments for sophisticated interiors.

That feels too vague for a practical local customer.
