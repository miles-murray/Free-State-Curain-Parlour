# Trust Signals

## What Customers Need To Trust

Customers buying curtains, blinds, furniture, and decor are usually making a considered purchase. They need to trust the business before they spend money or visit.

The website should answer these unspoken questions:

- Is this a real local shop?
- Can I visit and see things in person?
- Do they know what they are doing?
- Will they help me choose?
- Do they sell quality products?
- Can I contact them easily?
- Are other customers happy with them?
- Can they handle custom curtain work?

The "Est: 1959" detail is especially valuable. It signals longevity and should appear near the hero or in a short trust strip.

## Strongest Trust Signals For This Business

### Real Showroom Photos

Use real photos from the shop, not generic stock images. This is one of the most important trust signals because the business depends on walk-ins and local presence.

Show:

- Wide showroom views
- Curtain displays
- Furniture displays
- Fabric sample areas
- Counter or consultation area
- Exterior/front of shop if available

### Product Detail Photos

Use close-ups to show quality.

Good details:

- Fabric texture
- Curtain stitching
- Curtain rails/hooks
- Upholstery texture
- Couch stitching
- Cabinet handles
- Wood grain
- Cushions and finishes

### Staff And Service Photos

Show people helping people.

Best examples:

- Staff helping choose fabric
- Staff arranging cushions or displays
- A consultation moment at a table
- Measuring or installation photo, if they offer it

This makes the business feel human and approachable.

### Clear Location Information

Because walk-ins are important, location must be obvious.

Include:

- Westdene, Bloemfontein
- 44 Second Avenue, Westdene, Bloemfontein, 9301
- Map
- Parking or entrance photo if useful
- Trading hours
- Call and WhatsApp buttons

### Reviews And Testimonials

Use real reviews where possible. Prioritize comments that mention:

- Helpful staff
- Good service
- Decorating advice
- Quality products
- Friendly experience

Short reviews are fine. They do not need to be long.

### Local Language And Local References

Use Bloemfontein and Free State in the copy. Local buyers should feel the site is speaking to them.

Examples:

- "Visit our Westdene showroom"
- "Serving Bloemfontein homes"
- "Curtains, blinds and decor in the Free State"

### Before And After / Finished Rooms

If available, these are very strong for curtains and decor.

They prove:

- The work looks good installed
- The customer can imagine the result
- The team can coordinate a full room

### Clear Product Categories

People should quickly understand what they can get.

Recommended categories:

- Curtains
- Blinds
- Wallpaper
- Furniture
- Fabrics
- Decor
- Interior Design
- Soft furnishings
- Measuring and installation, if confirmed

## Trust Signals To Avoid Or Use Carefully

- Do not overuse generic stock photos.
- Do not make claims like "best in Bloemfontein" unless the client can support it.
- Do not list services they have not confirmed.
- Do not make the site feel like online checkout is available if it is mainly showroom-based.
- Do not hide the phone number or address.

## Best Conversion Path

The best next step is not "Buy Now."

The best next steps are:

- Visit the showroom
- Call the shop
- WhatsApp us
- Request a quote
- Ask for advice

Recommended CTA wording:

- Visit Our Showroom
- Get A Quote
- Call Us
- WhatsApp Us
- View Our Work
