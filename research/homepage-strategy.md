# Homepage Strategy

## Website Type

This should be a boutique local interiors showroom website.

It is similar in category to a furniture showroom website, but the personality should be more decor-led and guidance-led because curtains, blinds, fabrics, and room finishing are central to the business.

The website should not feel like a big e-commerce store. It should feel like a trusted local showroom that customers can visit for help, advice, and quality products.

## Main Homepage Goal

Get visitors to:

- Visit the showroom
- Call the shop
- WhatsApp the team
- Request a quote
- Understand the product categories
- Trust the business before walking in

## Primary Homepage Message

> Curtains, furniture and decor to help you complete your home.

Alternative:

> Visit our Bloemfontein showroom for curtains, blinds, furniture, fabrics and decor advice.

The supplied card also gives strong official language:

> Interior Design - Decor - Wallpaper - Blinds

This should influence the homepage. Curtains remain central because of the business name, but the homepage should clearly include interior design, decor, wallpaper, and blinds in the first screen or immediately below it.

## Recommended Homepage Sections

### 1. Hero

Purpose: Immediately explain who they are and what they do.

Suggested headline:

> Curtains, Furniture & Decor in Bloemfontein

Alternative using the official card language:

> Interior Design, Curtains, Decor, Wallpaper & Blinds

Suggested supporting copy:

> Free State Curtain Parlour helps you complete your space with curtains, blinds, wallpaper, decor, furniture and practical design advice from our Westdene showroom.

Calls to action:

- Visit Our Showroom
- Get a Quote
- Call / WhatsApp

Use the strongest showroom or furniture-plus-curtains image here.

### 2. Service Categories

Purpose: Quickly show the range.

Recommended categories:

- Custom Curtains
- Blinds
- Wallpaper
- Furniture
- Fabrics
- Decor & Interior Design
- Measuring & Installation, if confirmed

Each category should have a short one-line description and a real photo.

### 4. Curtains And Blinds

Purpose: Give their core service enough weight.

Mention:

- Made-to-measure curtains
- Sheer curtains
- Blockout curtains
- Curtain rods and rails
- Blinds
- Fabric options
- Advice on colour, texture, privacy, and light control

### 5. Furniture And Decor

Purpose: Broaden the business beyond curtains.

Mention:

- Couches
- Cabinets
- Lounge furniture
- Bedroom or dining furniture, if relevant
- Wallpaper
- Decor pieces
- Finishing touches

Messaging:

> Choose furniture, wallpaper and decor pieces that work with your curtains, fabrics and room style.

### 6. Help Choosing

Purpose: Sell their advice, not only their stock.

Messaging:

> Not sure what will work in your room? Our team helps you compare fabrics, colours, curtain styles and furniture pieces so your space feels complete.

This is important because customers may walk in uncertain. Advice is a conversion tool.

### 7. Gallery

Purpose: Let customers judge the shop visually.

Include:

- Cabinets
- Curtain displays
- Fabric close-ups
- Product details
- Staff/service moments
- Finished room examples if available

Real showroom photos were taken on 2026-08-17 and should be reviewed for homepage, category, and gallery use.

Trust points:

- Quality fabrics and finishes
- Custom curtain options
- Friendly service

Use real customer comments where possible. Keep them short.

## Design Direction

The visual style should be:

- Warm
- Elegant
- Tactile
- Showroom-based
- Local and trustworthy
- Slightly premium but approachable

Use real photography heavily. Avoid making it look like a generic online furniture catalogue.

Good visual ingredients:

- Fabric textures
- Warm neutrals
- Deep accent colours
- Real furniture displays
- Curtains in full length
- Human service moments
- Clean product grouping

## What The Homepage Must Prove

The homepage must prove:

- They are real and local.
- They have a physical showroom.
- They sell more than curtains.
- They can help customers choose.
- Their products look good in real spaces.
- They are easy to contact.
